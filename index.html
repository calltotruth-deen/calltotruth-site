Our Method

Why Trust These Reminders

Call To Truth exists for one purpose: to call hearts back to Allah through authentic reminders. We never use weak or fabricated narrations, never mix truth with speculation, and never present disputed matters as settled fact.

Every script passes two gates before it is ever filmed — Islamic accuracy (each verse checked against quran.com, each hadith against sunnah.com) and technical quality (4K picture, loud clear narration, captions synced to the voice).
