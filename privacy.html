Privacy Policy

Call To Truth — Last updated October 22, 2026

1. What we collect

Call To Truth is a da'wah website. We do not run accounts, comments, newsletters, or stores on this site. We collect no personal information directly.

2. Third-party platforms

Our videos are hosted on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest. When you visit those platforms via our links, their own privacy policies apply.

3. Analytics

This site uses no tracking cookies and no analytics scripts. GitHub Pages (our host) may log basic technical data as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

4. Contact

If you contact us by email, we use your message only to reply. We never sell or share personal data.

5. Children

This site is general-audience Islamic content and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Pinterest App Data Practices

The Call To Truth Pinterest app ("Call To Truth Publisher") is a first-party, single-user tool operated by Muhammad Umair, the owner of the Pinterest business account @calltotruth. It is used only by the account owner to publish the owner's own original video Pins to the owner's own Pinterest business account, using Pinterest's official OAuth login only. It has no other users.

Data accessed: only the owner's own Pinterest boards and Pins, via the Pinterest API (used to place Pins and avoid duplicates). The app collects, stores, or shares no data about any other person.

Data stored: the app's own Pinterest OAuth access and refresh tokens, and a record of the owner's own published Pins (Pin IDs, titles, and descriptions), kept on the owner's own computer. Tokens and records are never sold or shared with any third party.

Deletion: to request deletion of any stored app data, email awesomestory98@gmail.com (the @calltotruth account email). On request, OAuth tokens are revoked and stored records are deleted.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version will always be posted on this page.
